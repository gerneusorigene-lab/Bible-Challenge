Bible Challenge — Account Deletion

Belleus Educational Games

This page explains how users of Bible Challenge can request deletion of their account and associated account information.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to your Bible Challenge account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to the Account section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. For security, you may be required to sign in again before Firebase allows the account to be deleted.

Request deletion outside the app

If you cannot access the app or your account, you may request account deletion by contacting Belleus Educational Games using the developer contact information published with Bible Challenge on its official app-store listing. Please identify the request as a Bible Challenge account deletion request and provide the email address associated with the account so the request can be verified and processed.

Data deleted

When the Bible Challenge sign-in account is deleted, the Firebase Authentication account associated with that user is deleted. This includes the authentication identity associated with the account, such as its email address and any display name stored with that Firebase Authentication profile.

Data that may be retained separately

Account deletion does not necessarily delete transaction, subscription, or purchase records maintained separately by Google Play, RevenueCat, or other payment-platform providers. Those providers may retain records according to their own policies and applicable legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record requirements.

Game progress or preferences stored only on a user's device may also remain on that device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed.

Need help?

Use the developer contact information shown on the official Bible Challenge store listing and specify that your message concerns account deletion.
